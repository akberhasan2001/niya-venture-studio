# Public portfolio research

Researched October 27, 2026. These are source-based editorial profiles, not
company-approved biographies, investment recommendations or statements of Niya's
ownership. Taqi's normal pre-launch content review remains outstanding.

## VePay

- https://vepay.io/en/about-us/ — mission, leadership roles, MBA/PhD credentials.
- https://vepay.io/en/ — eCommerce factoring and working-capital products.
- Correct company: VePay Finance, led by Mafaz Jaufer and Souhail Meftah. Other
  similarly named payment platforms were excluded.
- Founder copy is intentionally brief: the official About page establishes roles
  and credentials but does not provide detailed individual career biographies.

- https://www.halalwallet.us/about — education/comparison platform, founding story.
- https://www.halalwallet.us/authors/kyle-natter — consumer lending background,
  partnerships, product evaluation, editorial role and IFN writing.
- https://www.halalwallet.us/authors/robert-mallon — product strategy, methodology,
  education, Deloitte and fintech background.

  verified downloadable Robert Mallon portrait was found. His card uses initials.

  not a bank, lender or direct provider of the products it compares.

## Ummah Unite
