One day, two tracks, many conversations.

Niya Venture Studio and the Silicon Valley Entrepreneurship Club co-organized the Summit at the Computer History Museum. The program included founder and investor conversations, a startup pitch competition, an all-day hackathon and an expo floor.

What came through clearly

The event was built to connect people who are building for the global Muslim economy with people who can help them build for the world. The next editions will build on that foundation.

“The quality of the conversations and the genuine willingness to support one another made the event truly special.”
